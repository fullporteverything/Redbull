04 — The story

Chapter 01 — 1987

Born by a lake.

The first cans leave a small office in Fuschl am See, Austria. Nobody has a shelf for it yet, so it makes its own.

Chapter 02 — 1997

Crossing the Atlantic.

After a decade spreading through Europe, the slim can lands in California. It arrives quietly and does not stay quiet for long.

Chapter 03 — 2012

Thirty-nine kilometres up.

A capsule, a balloon and one step off the edge of the stratosphere, watched live by millions of people holding their breath.

Chapter 04 — Today

Still the same can.

Sold in 177 countries, and the 250 ml original is still the one most people reach for first.
